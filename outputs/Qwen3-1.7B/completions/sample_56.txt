3. **Iterative Distribution**: We iterate through each engineer, calculating the maximum possible contribution they can make given the remaining work. This is done by taking the minimum of the remaining work and the maximum possible contribution based on their benefit formula.

4. **Output**: The contributions of each engineer are printed in the required format.
