### Approach
1. **Identify Median and Next Higher Element**: Calculate the median `m` of the array `a` and determine the next higher element for elements that are equal to `m`.
2. **Group Elements**: Classify elements into two groups based on whether they are part of the median (`m` group) or not (`m` group).
3. **Calculate Contributions**: For each element, calculate its contribution to the score if incremented up to the maximum allowed operations.
4. **Maximize Contributions**: Select the elements with the highest contributions and apply the maximum allowed increments to them to achieve the highest possible score.
