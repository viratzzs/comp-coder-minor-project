### Approach

2. **Median Calculation**: The median of an array of length `n` is the `(n+1)//2`-th element in the sorted array.
3. **Query Processing**: For each query value `x`, determine the number of elements less than `x` in the sorted array. This helps in identifying the range of elements that will be reduced.
4. **Efficient Reduction Calculation**: For elements greater than or equal to `x`, compute their reduced values by repeatedly subtracting `x` until the element becomes less than `x`. This helps in determining the median after all reductions.
